browser-qchem / milestone 03

Hartree-Fock, in your browser.

Closed-shell molecular energies, SCF convergence, orbitals, and complete integrals. Computed locally with libcint, Eigen, and WebAssembly.

Geometry

STO-3G for H through Ne, maximum 40 AOs. RHF uses paired electrons and spin 0. Integrals-only mode also accepts odd-electron molecules.
