browser-qchem / milestone 02

Molecular integrals, in your browser.

Full overlap, kinetic, nuclear attraction, and electron repulsion integrals. Computed locally with libcint and WebAssembly.

Geometry

STO-3G basis. Supported elements: H through Ne. Maximum 40 AOs. Nuclear charges come from the elements; electron count and spin are not needed for these integrals.

AO order and array layout

      
JSON arrays are row-major. Matrix index: i*N + j.
        ERI index: ((i*N + j)*N + k)*N + l, with zero-based indices in the file.
        Exported coordinates are in bohr. S is dimensionless; all energy quantities use atomic units.
